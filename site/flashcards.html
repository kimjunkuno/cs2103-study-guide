How it works · saved cards & backups

Each card tests one short bold or highlighted statement from the main topic content. Dimmed text, examples and supplementary panels are excluded. Source statements are at most 35 words; longer points are left in the study guide rather than cut mid-explanation. Source wording is available in the collapsed section after reveal. These are study questions, not official exam questions. Linked external lessons are not copied. Revisited weeks retain their assigned material.

Allow random repeats lets random selection reuse seen cards; with it off, each question appears once per session. Repeat this card explicitly hides the current answer so you can try it again. Changing week, topic or Revisit only starts a fresh session without clearing your saved cards.

Skip & save and Got it wrong save questions for later. Got it right removes a question from the revisit list. Revealing or choosing Next does not grade the answer. Use Revisit only to practise saved questions. No automatic grading or review schedule is used.

Saved flags stay in this browser. Private browsing, clearing storage or changing browsers can lose them. Export a backup to keep them. Import replaces the list after validation; unknown question IDs are ignored. Study-guide confidence and flashcard flags are separate. Existing flags are retained for matching cards; cards removed from this core-only deck no longer appear in the revisit list.
