How it works · saved cards & backups

Each generated question hides a term or phrase from a bolded theory passage. The answer and full source passage use the website’s wording. These are study questions, not official exam questions. Topics with no suitable bolded text and linked external lessons are not included. Revisited weeks retain their own assigned questions.

Allow random repeats lets random selection reuse seen cards; with it off, each question appears once per session. Repeat this card explicitly hides the current answer so you can try it again. Changing week, topic or Revisit only starts a fresh session without clearing your saved cards.

Skip & save and Got it wrong save questions for later. Got it right removes a question from the revisit list. Revealing or choosing Next does not grade the answer. Use Revisit only to practise saved questions. No automatic grading or review schedule is used.

Saved flags stay in this browser. Private browsing, clearing storage or changing browsers can lose them. Export a backup to keep them. Import replaces the list after validation; unknown question IDs are ignored. Study-guide confidence and flashcard flags are separate.
